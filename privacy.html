Privacy

What we keep, and why

Last updated 7 November 2026. Owner to review before launch.

Who we are

SunDestin Online is the owner's own website for SunDestin #203 at Sun Destin Beach Resort, Destin, Florida. The owner is the only person who sees anything you send through it. Contact: .

What this site collects

  • Inquiries. When you send an inquiry, your email app sends the owner your name, email, dates, guest count and message. The site itself stores nothing.
  • Account (demo). If you register, your name, email and a scrambled form of your password are stored in your own browser only, not on a server. You can delete the account on the account page at any time.
  • Preferences. Your colour theme choice is kept in your browser.

What this site does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking cookies.
  • No card or payment details. Any payment is arranged directly with the owner, outside this site.
  • No sharing or selling of your details to anyone.

Outside services the page loads

  • Google Maps for the location map and Google Fonts for the typeface. Google may record your IP address when these load. See Google's privacy policy.
  • The site is hosted on Hostinger, which keeps standard server logs (IP address, pages requested) for security.

Your choices

Write to the owner to ask what they hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Inquiry emails are deleted when no longer needed for a stay or for tax records.
